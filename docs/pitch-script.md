# Pitch Script — Final (Aug 2026)

## Opener (the question)
> "Hey — I saw your restaurant on Google Maps, looks really great. But I searched for your website and couldn't find one. Have you tried to get one but didn't know where to look?"

Let them talk. Their story tells you what they want.

## The reveal
> "So I did something a little crazy — I already prebuilt one for you. Check this out."

Hand them the phone. Silence while they scroll.

## The close
- If impressed: "It's yours. Live in 3 days."
- Price when asked: "2 500 MAD total — half now, half when it's live. First month of hosting free, then 300 MAD/month."

## Objection: "If I pay you, what guarantees me?"
1. **You've already seen the finished product.** You're not paying for a promise — the site is built, you're looking at it.
2. **Half now, half at delivery.** If I don't deliver the site live in 3 days, you don't pay the second half.
3. **Domain and hosting in YOUR name.** From day one everything is registered under the restaurant's name. If we ever stop working together, the site stays yours.
4. **First month free.** If maintenance isn't worth 300 MAD to you, cancel — no contract, no penalty.

## Other objections
- **"Too expensive"** → "One table a month pays for it. And people who find you online become tables you never had."
- **"My nephew will do it"** → "Good — but when? Mine is done today. If his turns out better, keep his."
- **"Not now / later"** → "No problem. The demo stays yours either way — my number's on the back. When 'later' comes, call me."
- **"Show me results first"** → hand phone over again; the demo IS the result.
